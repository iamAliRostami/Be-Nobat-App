\# 1. Executive Summary



\## معرفی



«به نوبت» یک پلتفرم جامع مدیریت نوبت، منابع و ارتباط با مشتری (Appointment \& Resource Management Platform) است که برای تمامی کسب‌وکارهایی طراحی شده است که ارائه خدمات آن‌ها وابسته به زمان، منابع یا پرسنل است.



این محصول صرفاً یک سیستم رزرو آنلاین نیست؛ بلکه هدف آن ایجاد بستری هوشمند برای مدیریت فرآیند ارائه خدمات، کاهش کارهای دستی، افزایش بهره‌وری و بهبود تجربه مشتری است.



این سیستم باید به گونه‌ای طراحی شود که از یک آرایشگاه کوچک تا یک درمانگاه، کلینیک، آموزشگاه، مرکز مشاوره، تعمیرگاه، سالن زیبایی، استودیو، باشگاه ورزشی یا هر مجموعه خدماتی دیگر بتواند بدون تغییر در معماری اصلی از آن استفاده کند.



\---



\## چشم‌انداز کلی



در بسیاری از کسب‌وکارهای خدماتی، فرآیند نوبت‌دهی هنوز به صورت تلفنی، پیام‌رسان، دفتر کاغذی یا نرم‌افزارهای محدود انجام می‌شود.



این موضوع باعث ایجاد مشکلات زیر می‌شود:



\- فراموش شدن نوبت‌ها

\- اشتباه در ثبت زمان

\- تداخل نوبت‌ها

\- زمان‌های مرده بین نوبت‌ها

\- وابستگی شدید به اپراتور

\- عدم امکان گزارش‌گیری

\- نبود تحلیل عملکرد

\- نبود ارتباط مناسب با مشتری



هدف «به نوبت» حذف این مشکلات است.

\# 2. Vision



\## چشم‌انداز محصول



هدف نهایی "به نوبت" ساخت هوشمندترین پلتفرم مدیریت خدمات زمان‌محور در ایران است؛ پلتفرمی که بتواند فرآیند مدیریت مشتری، منابع، نوبت‌ها و عملیات روزانه کسب‌وکارها را تا حد ممکن خودکار کند.



در چشم‌انداز نهایی، یک کسب‌وکار بتواند بدون نیاز به حضور دائمی یک اپراتور، فعالیت روزانه خود را مدیریت کند.



سیستم باید بتواند:



\- بهترین زمان‌های خالی را پیشنهاد دهد.

\- زمان‌های پرت را کاهش دهد.

\- مشتریان را به انتخاب زمان‌های مناسب‌تر هدایت کند.

\- مدیریت انتظار را انجام دهد.

\- اعلان‌ها را به صورت خودکار ارسال کند.

\- بخشی از تصمیم‌های روزمره مدیر را خودکار نماید.

\- در آینده از تحلیل داده و هوش مصنوعی برای بهبود عملکرد استفاده کند.



\---



\## جمله چشم‌انداز



> به نوبت فقط یک سیستم نوبت‌دهی نیست؛ بلکه دستیار هوشمند مدیریت کسب‌وکارهای خدماتی است.

\# 3. Mission



ماموریت اصلی محصول ایجاد بستری است که:



\- تجربه رزرو نوبت را برای مشتری بسیار ساده کند.

\- مدیریت مجموعه را برای مدیران آسان کند.

\- بهره‌وری منابع را افزایش دهد.

\- زمان‌های بدون استفاده را کاهش دهد.

\- ارتباط میان مشتری و ارائه‌دهنده خدمت را بهبود دهد.

\- اطلاعات ارزشمند برای تصمیم‌گیری تولید کند.

\# 4. Product Philosophy



طراحی این محصول بر اساس چند اصل غیرقابل تغییر انجام می‌شود.



\---



\## اصل اول



\### سیستم نباید آزادی انتخاب کاربر را محدود کند.



به جای اجبار، سیستم باید پیشنهاد هوشمند ارائه دهد.



مثال:



اگر ساعت 14:00 باعث پر شدن یک فاصله خالی شود، سیستم آن را پیشنهاد می‌دهد؛ اما کاربر همچنان می‌تواند ساعت 16:00 را انتخاب کند.



\---



\## اصل دوم



\### هر تصمیم باید قابل تنظیم باشد.



تقریباً هیچ قانونی نباید به صورت Hard Code داخل سیستم وجود داشته باشد.



نمونه‌ها:



\- مدت زمان لغو نوبت

\- نیاز یا عدم نیاز به تأیید

\- فاصله بین نوبت‌ها

\- قوانین پرداخت

\- قوانین امتیازدهی



همگی باید از طریق تنظیمات هر Business قابل مدیریت باشند.



\---



\## اصل سوم



\### سیستم باید برای هر نوع کسب‌وکار قابل استفاده باشد.



محصول نباید مخصوص:



\- پزشک

\- آرایشگاه

\- باشگاه



یا هر شغل خاص دیگری طراحی شود.



بلکه باید به اندازه کافی Generic باشد که هر کسب‌وکار خدماتی بتواند از آن استفاده کند.



\---



\## اصل چهارم



\### Staff نیز یک Resource است.



در معماری سیستم، پرسنل یک نوع Resource محسوب می‌شود.



این تصمیم باعث می‌شود در آینده بتوان تجهیزات، اتاق‌ها، دستگاه‌ها و سایر منابع را نیز بدون تغییر معماری وارد سیستم کرد.



\---



\## اصل پنجم



\### حذف اطلاعات ممنوع است.



هیچ اطلاعات مهمی به صورت فیزیکی حذف نمی‌شود.



تمام داده‌ها باید قابلیت بازیابی، گزارش‌گیری و Audit داشته باشند.



\---



\## اصل ششم



\### سیستم باید آینده‌نگر باشد.



در نسخه اول شاید بسیاری از قابلیت‌های هوشمند پیاده‌سازی نشوند؛ اما معماری باید از ابتدا برای آن‌ها آماده باشد.



نمونه‌ها:

\- Dynamic Pricing

\- Reputation Engine

# 7. Target Audience

## Overview

Be Nobat is designed as a generic appointment and resource management platform. It is not limited to a specific industry and should support any business where services are provided based on time, staff, equipment, or other reservable resources.

The platform is intended to scale from small local businesses to enterprise organizations with multiple branches.

---

## Primary Target Audience

The primary customers of Be Nobat are businesses that provide appointment-based services.

Examples include:

- Medical Clinics
- Dental Clinics
- Hospitals
- Physiotherapy Centers
- Beauty Salons
- Barber Shops
- Spa Centers
- Gyms
- Educational Institutes
- Driving Schools
- Photography Studios
- Repair Centers
- Legal Consultants
- Financial Consultants
- Coaching Centers
- Veterinary Clinics
- Government Appointment Centers

The architecture must remain independent of any specific business domain.

---

## Secondary Target Audience

Although businesses are the paying customers, the platform must provide an excellent experience for end users as well.

These include:

- Customers
- Employees
- Freelancers
- Branch Managers
- Business Owners
- System Administrators

Every type of user should have a dedicated experience based on their responsibilities.

---

# 8. Stakeholders

The product contains multiple stakeholders with different expectations.

---

## Customer

The customer wants to:

- Book appointments easily.
- Receive reminders.
- Find suitable service providers.
- Compare available times.
- Track appointment history.
- Review businesses.
- Save favorite businesses.
- Receive loyalty rewards.

Success for the customer means spending less time booking and managing appointments.

---

## Staff

Staff members want to:

- View their schedules.
- Manage appointments.
- Request leave.
- View customer information.
- Receive ratings.
- Improve their reputation.

The system should minimize manual coordination.

---

## Branch Manager

Branch managers are responsible for daily operations.

Responsibilities include:

- Managing staff
- Managing working hours
- Managing appointments
- Managing leaves
- Managing services
- Monitoring branch performance

---

## Business Owner

Business owners require strategic insights rather than operational details.

Typical expectations include:

- Revenue reports
- Staff productivity
- Customer retention
- Occupancy rate
- Business growth
- Branch comparison

---

## Platform Administrator

The platform administrator manages the SaaS platform itself.

Responsibilities include:

- Creating Businesses
- Managing subscriptions
- Platform monitoring
- Global settings
- Security
- User moderation

# 10. Product Value Proposition

Be Nobat delivers value through four major pillars.

---

## Simplicity

Booking appointments should require only a few steps.

Users should never feel overwhelmed.

---

## Flexibility

Every business has different rules.

Instead of hardcoding business logic, almost every behavior should be configurable.

Examples include:

- Cancellation policy
- Approval workflow
- Working hours
- Payment rules
- Gap between appointments
- Loyalty rules
- Reputation rules

---

## Scalability

The architecture should support:

- Single branch businesses
- Multi-branch organizations
- Thousands of daily appointments
- Millions of customers

without redesigning the system.

---

## Intelligence

The platform should continuously evolve toward intelligent decision making.

Examples:

- Smart Scheduling
- Waiting List Optimization
- AI Recommendations
- Demand Prediction
- Automatic Resource Assignment
- Dynamic Pricing

These features are part of the long-term vision but must be considered during architecture design.

---

# 11. Competitive Advantages

Be Nobat differentiates itself through several key principles.

## Generic Architecture

The platform is industry-independent.

Most competitors are designed specifically for medical centers or beauty salons.

Be Nobat is designed for every appointment-based business.

---

## Resource-Based Scheduling

Appointments are not limited to staff.

Any reservable entity is considered a Resource.

Examples:

- Employee
- Room
- Chair
- Device
- Vehicle

This makes future expansion significantly easier.

---

## Smart Scheduling Engine

Instead of displaying appointments randomly, available time slots receive a scheduling score.

Customers are encouraged—not forced—to choose the most efficient slots.

Benefits include:

- Reduced idle time
- Increased occupancy
- Better customer distribution

---

## Reputation System

The platform includes both:

- Global Reputation
- Business Reputation

allowing businesses to make better decisions while maintaining a platform-wide trust score.

---

## Automation First

Every repetitive task should eventually become automated.

Examples include:

- Notifications
- Waiting List
- Appointment Confirmation
- Reminder Messages
- Calendar Optimization

# 12. Business Model

## Platform Type

Be Nobat is designed as a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform.

The platform itself owns the infrastructure while businesses subscribe to use the services.

The system should support thousands of independent businesses running on a shared platform.

---

## Revenue Model

The primary revenue model is subscription-based.

Possible subscription plans include:

- Free Trial
- Basic
- Professional
- Enterprise

Future monetization options may include:

- SMS Packages
- AI Features
- Premium Reports
- Online Payment Commission
- Marketplace Services
- API Access
- White Label Solutions

---

## Customer Acquisition Strategy

The initial target market consists of small and medium-sized businesses.

The long-term goal is to become the default appointment management platform for service-based businesses.

Growth channels may include:

- Direct Sales
- Referral Programs
- Digital Marketing
- Partnerships
- App Stores
- Business Associations

# 15. Non-Functional Goals

The platform should satisfy the following quality attributes.

## Performance

- Fast response time
- Efficient appointment calculation
- High availability

---

## Scalability

Support growth from:

- One business
- Hundreds of businesses
- Thousands of branches
- Millions of appointments

without architectural redesign.

---

## Reliability

The platform should remain stable even under heavy load.

Critical operations such as appointment booking must prevent race conditions and double booking.

---

## Security

The platform should protect:

- Personal Information
- Appointment Data
- Business Information
- Payment Information

Role-based authorization must be enforced throughout the system.

---

## Extensibility

Every major module should be replaceable or extendable.

Future features should require minimal architectural changes.

---

## Auditability

Every critical business action should be traceable.

Examples:

- Appointment Created
- Appointment Cancelled
- Appointment Rescheduled
- Staff Assignment Changed
- Business Settings Modified

# 17. Product Strategy

The product evolves through four maturity stages.

---

## Phase 1 — Digitalization

Objective:

Replace manual appointment management.

Examples:

- Booking
- Calendar
- Notifications
- Customer Management

---

## Phase 2 — Optimization

Objective:

Increase operational efficiency.

Examples:

- Smart Scheduling
- Waiting List
- Reputation
- Loyalty
- Gap Optimization

---

## Phase 3 — Automation

Objective:

Reduce human intervention.

Examples:

- Automatic Confirmations
- Automatic Notifications
- Automatic Resource Assignment
- Smart Calendar Optimization

---

## Phase 4 — Intelligence

Objective:

Transform the platform into an intelligent business assistant.

Examples:

- AI Scheduling
- Demand Prediction
- Business Insights

- Customer Behavior Prediction
- Intelligent Recommendations

# 18. Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective of Be Nobat is not merely to digitize appointment booking.

The platform should become an intelligent operating system for service-based businesses.

Managers should spend less time on operational tasks and more time improving their business.

Customers should experience appointment booking as a fast, reliable and enjoyable process.

Ultimately, the platform should become capable of making data-driven recommendations and automating routine decisions while keeping business owners in full control.

---

# 19. Product Principles

Every future decision should align with these principles.

1. Customer experience comes first.
2. Configuration is preferred over hardcoding.
3. Simplicity is better than complexity.
4. Automation is better than manual work.
5. Data should support every decision.
6. Resources are first-class citizens.
7. The platform should guide users instead of restricting them.
8. Every important action should be auditable.
9. Future expansion should never require redesign.
10. The platform must remain business-agnostic.

# 20. Glossary

|------|-------------|

| Customer | Person receiving services |

# 21. Conclusion

Be Nobat is envisioned as a next-generation appointment and resource management platform.

Its architecture is intentionally designed to support businesses of different sizes and industries while remaining flexible, scalable and future-ready.

Rather than focusing solely on appointment booking, the platform aims to optimize business operations, improve customer experience and progressively automate repetitive processes.

Every architectural decision made throughout the project should support this long-term vision.

The ultimate success of Be Nobat will not be measured by the number of appointments booked, but by how effectively it enables businesses to operate with greater efficiency, fewer manual processes and higher customer satisfaction.
